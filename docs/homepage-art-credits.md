# Homepage artwork credits

The following historical images are bundled locally for the homepage. They are decorative archival references; neither depicts Sir Regret.

- **“Globe with Mapping Instruments”**, engraved by Etienne Fessard after Edme Bouchardon, 18th century. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 41.44.1224. Public Domain; The Met Open Access permits unrestricted commercial and noncommercial use. [Collection record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/717215). Local file: `public/art/archives/globe-mapping-instruments.jpg`.
- **“The Kitchen”**, anonymous engraving, 1842, from *Merry’s Museum*. Public domain as an anonymous work published in 1842. [Wikimedia Commons record and rights details](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Kitchen_1842.jpg). Local file: `public/art/archives/kitchen-1842.jpg`.

The museum-front, teacup, department-map, curiosity-shelf, and contribution-desk illustrations in `public/art/storybook/` were created for this project and are original artwork.
